# storyline.md

Presentation-side mechanics of the headline spine: the sequence of
slide titles, read on its own with no body content, must function as
a linear argument.

## The load-bearing rule: 1 slide, 1 message + headline-only test

Every slide carries exactly one headline stating one claim. Extract
the headlines in slide order, read them straight through with no
other context, and the sequence must read as a coherent argument
the reader could act on. If it does not, the spine is broken; fix
the spine before touching any slide's composition. A headline whose
claim needs the word "and" to hold two ideas together should be
split into two slides.

## What lives in the `deck-storyline` skill (not here)

Governing question, storyline design, evidence architecture,
epistemic labeling ("we believe" vs "we measured"), action-title
construction, audience-fit, and the full 8-step workflow are owned
by the `deck-storyline` skill. Invoke that skill for any deep
storyline work. This file only covers the presentation-side
mechanics that slide composition must enforce.
